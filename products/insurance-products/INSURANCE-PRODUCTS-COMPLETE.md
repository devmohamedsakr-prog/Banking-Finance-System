# Insurance Products - Complete Guide

**Premium:** $500B+ annually | **Customers:** 200M+ | **Claims Paid:** 99%

## Insurance Types

### Life Insurance

**Term Life (20-30 year)**
- Death benefit: $100K-$1M
- Monthly premium: $15-$50
- Payout: Tax-free to beneficiary
- No cash value

**Whole Life**
- Death benefit: $100K-$500K
- Monthly premium: $100-$300
- Cash value: 70% of premium after year 1
- Lifetime coverage

### Auto Insurance

**Liability**
- Bodily injury: $100K-$500K
- Property damage: $100K-$500K
- Premium: $500-$1,500/year

**Collision & Comprehensive**
- Deductible: $250-$1K
- Covers: Accidents, theft, weather
- Premium: $200-$800/year

**Uninsured Motorist**
- Covers: Accidents with uninsured drivers
- Premium: $50-$200/year

### Home Insurance

**Homeowner Policy**
- Dwelling: Replacement cost
- Contents: 70% of dwelling
- Liability: $100K-$500K
- Deductible: $500-$2.5K
- Premium: $1K-$3K/year

**Flood Insurance**
- Separate policy
- Coverage: Up to $250K dwelling
- Premium: $500-$3K/year
- Waiting period: 30 days

### Health Insurance

**Bronze:** 60% coverage, $200-$400/mo
**Silver:** 70% coverage, $300-$500/mo
**Gold:** 80% coverage, $400-$700/mo
**Platinum:** 90% coverage, $600-$900/mo

### Travel Insurance

- Trip protection: $50-$200 per trip
- Annual pass: $300-$500
- Coverage: Cancellation, delay, medical
- Max reimbursement: $10K
