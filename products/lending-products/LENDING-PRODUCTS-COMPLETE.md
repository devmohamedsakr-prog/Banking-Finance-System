# Lending Products - Complete Guide

**Portfolio:** $10T | **Active Loans:** 100M+ | **APR Range:** 6-36%

## Loan Types

### Personal Loan
- Amount: $1K-$50K
- Term: 12-60 months
- APR: 8-36% (credit-based)
- Approval: <5 minutes
- Use: Debt consolidation, home improvement

### Business Loan
- Amount: $10K-$1M
- Term: 1-7 years
- APR: 6-18% (revenue-based)
- SBA-backed options
- Use: Working capital, equipment

### Auto Loan
- Amount: $5K-$100K
- Term: 36-72 months
- APR: 4-10% (vehicle/credit-based)
- Rate locks: 30 days
- Use: New/used vehicle

### Mortgage
- Amount: $50K-$1M+
- Term: 10/15/20/30 years
- APR: 3-8% (market-based)
- Rate lock: 45-60 days
- Closing: 7-10 days

### Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)
- Amount: $50-$5K (at checkout)
- Term: 4-12 weeks
- Fee: $0 if on-time, 25% APR if late
- Instant approval
- E-commerce focused

### Credit Line
- Amount: $1K-$100K
- Term: Revolving (3-5 year review)
- APR: 7-25% (credit-based)
- Interest-only available
- Emergency funds

## Approval Workflow

1. Application (2 min)
2. Income verification (Plaid) (1 min)
3. Credit check (Equifax) (1 min)
4. ML scoring (1 min)
5. Decision (<5 min total)
