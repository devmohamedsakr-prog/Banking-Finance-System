# MiFID II - Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II

## Conduct of Business Rules

### Know Your Customer (KYC)

```
Information Required:

1. Customer Identity
   - Name
   - Date of birth
   - Address
   - Nationality
   - PEP status (politically exposed person)

2. Financial Situation
   - Income/salary
   - Net worth
   - Investment portfolio value
   - Employment status

3. Investment Experience
   - Years investing
   - Types of investments
   - Prior gains/losses
   - Knowledge level (novice to expert)

4. Investment Objectives
   - Risk tolerance (conservative to aggressive)
   - Investment horizon (1 year to 30 years)
   - Liquidity needs
   - Expected return

5. Suitability Assessment
   - Is investment appropriate?
   - Does it match profile?
   - Document in file
   - Obtain written consent
```

### Best Execution

```
Requirement:

Execute all orders at best terms available
- Price (most important)
- Speed
- Likelihood of settlement
- Size of order
- Other factors

Execution Venues:
- Stock exchange (highest volume)
- Alternative trading systems (ATS)
- Market makers
- Brokers

Documentation:
- Execution policy maintained
- Quarterly review
- Annual testing
- Transparency to clients
```

### Investment Advice

```
Suitability Report Required:

Contents:
1. Recommended investment
2. Why it's suitable
3. Risk explanation
4. Cost and fees
5. Performance assumptions

Example:
"We recommend a 60% stocks / 40% bonds allocation 
given your age 35, 25-year horizon, and MODERATE 
risk tolerance. This should generate 7% annual returns 
with 8% volatility."
```

## Reporting Requirements

```
Pre-Trade Transparency:
- Publish quotes (bid/ask prices)
- Update every 50 milliseconds
- Size information (minimum)

Post-Trade Reporting:
- Report every trade
- Within 1 minute of execution
- Trade ID, price, quantity, time
- Public accessible

Client Reporting:
- Monthly statements
- Annual performance report
- Trading costs breakdown
- Investment policy review
```

## Record Keeping

```
Retention: Minimum 5 years

Records to Keep:
- KYC documentation
- Suitability assessments
- Investment recommendations
- Trade confirmations
- Order records
- Call recordings
- Email correspondence
- Complaints and resolutions

Accessibility:
- Must be retrievable
- Auditable
- Timestamped
- Immutable
```

## Penalties

```
Civil:
- Up to €15M fine
- Or 5% of annual turnover
- Whichever is higher

Criminal:
- Up to 4 years imprisonment
- Additional fines

Examples:
- Goldman Sachs: €110M (market abuse)
- Barclays: €135M (interest rate manipulation)
```

## Status
✅ Mandatory | EU investment firms required
