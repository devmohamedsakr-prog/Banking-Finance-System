# TILA - Truth in Lending Disclosures

## Required Disclosures

### Before Consumer Commits (Loan Estimate)

**For Mortgage:**
```
1. Loan Amount: $300,000

2. Interest Rate: 6.5% (fixed)

3. Monthly Payment: $1,896
   - Principal & Interest: $1,896
   - Taxes & Insurance: Included if escrowed
   
4. Total Interest: $382,370
   (Over 30 years)

5. Finance Charge: $82,370
   (Interest + fees)

6. Total of All Payments: $682,370

7. Payment Schedule:
   - 360 monthly payments of $1,896
   - First payment due: 02/01/2024
   - Final payment due: 01/01/2054

8. Right to Rescind:
   - You have 3 days to cancel
   - For home purchase: NO rescission right
   - For refinance: YES (3-day right)
```

**For Credit Card:**
```
Annual Percentage Rate (APR):
- Purchase APR: 18.99%
- Balance transfer APR: 21.99%
- Cash advance APR: 25.99%

Annual Fee: $95

Grace Period: 25 days (purchases only)

Late Fee: $35

Penalty APR: 29.99% (triggered after 60+ days late)
```

**For Personal Loan:**
```
Amount: $10,000

Term: 36 months

APR: 12.5%

Finance Charge: $2,000

Total Amount Paid: $12,000

Monthly Payment: $333.33

Right to Prepay: Yes, no penalty
```

## APR Tolerance

```
Tolerance for APR Disclosure Error:

Mortgage Loans:
- Tolerance: ±0.125% APR
- $300K loan at 6.5%: Can disclose 6.375%-6.625%

Home Equity Lines (HELOC):
- Tolerance: ±0.25% APR

All Other Loans:
- Tolerance: ±0.125% APR
```

## Advertising Rules

```
If Ad Mentions Any Credit Term:

Example: "No interest if paid in full within 12 months!"

Must Also Disclose:
- APR
- Down payment
- Number of payments
- Monthly payment (if different terms)
- Finance charge
- How APR is calculated

Complete Example:
"No interest if paid in full within 12 months!
12 monthly payments of $83.33 ($1,000 purchase).
If not paid in full within 12 months, 
APR 22.99% applies (Finance charge: ~$275)"
```

## Penalties

```
Civil Liability:

Per Violation:
- Minimum: $100
- Maximum: $5,000
- Class action: Unlimited

Actual Damages:
- Greater of statutory penalty or actual damage
- Attorney fees
- Court costs

Criminal:
- Up to 5 years prison
- $5,000 fine
- For willful violations
```
